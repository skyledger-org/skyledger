Which facility is each plume coming from?

You'll see one satellite picture at a time and answer one question. About a minute each. SkyLedger's own answers are hidden here, so the test is fair. Afterwards it's scored against you.

  1. 1. Find the ✚ It marks where the plume starts. The smoke spreads out from there, the way the white arrow (the wind) points.
  2. 2. Is a lettered dot right there? Each dot is a facility that reports to the EPA. A dot at the ✚ is the source.
  3. 3. A dot the smoke only drifts toward is not the source No dot at the ✚? Pick None of these. Two equally likely, or can't see? Pick Not sure. That's a fine answer.
