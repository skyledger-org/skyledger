01 · The problem

Three sources that were never designed to meet.

kg / hour

What satellites see

A methane plume at a point, on one morning. Carbon Mapper (Tanager, EMIT, AVIRIS-NG, GAO).

t CO₂e / year

What companies report

One annual total per facility ID to the EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program.

free text

Who owns it

“TARGA RESOURCES (73%); PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES USA INC (27%)”: inconsistent strings, no IDs.

No shared keys, no shared units, no shared clock. Nobody reconciles them at scale, so a leak seen from space rarely meets the number a company reported.

02 · Who it's for, and why now

The people who must judge whether a reported number is plausible.

  • ESG and credit analysts signing off on issuers' emissions disclosures.
  • Regulators and state agencies prioritising inspections.
  • Investigative journalists and NGOs who need evidence, not anecdotes.

Why now

  • California SB 253: first Scope 1 and 2 reports due 10 Nov 2026, with no assurance requirement yet.
  • EPA has delayed RY2025 GHGRP reporting and proposed ending most of the program. Independent checks matter more.
  • Satellite methane data is now public and frequent (Carbon Mapper Tanager).

03 · The solution

Every plume becomes a traced, signed-off dossier.

Find

Federated geo-join of plumes to reporting facilities, queried in place through Zetaris.

Attribute

Wind and plume geometry decide which facility sits at the plume's head. Nemotron reads the image as a check.

Reconcile

kg/h → tonnes per year, always as a range, against the facility's report and its parent companies.

Challenge

A Skeptic must rule out five rival explanations before anything is written. An analyst signs before it can be shared.

Output: draft dossiers with ranges, evidence (SQL, image, computation), a parent-company roll-up, and a hash-chained trace of every agent step. Wording is guarded: “inconsistent with available data”, never an accusation.

Not a linear chain

The Skeptic sends work back: widen the search (GeoLinker), look again (PlumeReader), stop annualising (Reconciler). Loops are bounded by config.

Numbers come from code

Distances, unit conversion and ratios come from SQL and code. Models interpret and word; a guard rejects any number the code didn't compute.

Traceable

Every step goes to logs/trace-<run>.jsonl, hash-chained so any edit breaks the chain, and streams live to the console.

05 · The Skeptic

Before a word is written, five rival explanations must fall.

RivalThe questionHow it's tested (data, never opinion)
R1 MisattributionCould another facility be the source?Plume head vs. candidate distance and wind; basin-level operators nearby are flagged
R2 IntermittencyA one-off release?Sightings inside the reporting-year window; fewer than 2 blocks any annual figure
R3 Not a reporterIs the site required to report?Subparts filed, CH₄ reported, or no reporter at the plume head
R4 CoverageDoes the report cover this equipment?Basin-aggregate reports (W-ONSH/W-GB) and Subpart W coverage
R5 Data qualityIs the measurement usable?Rate present, uncertainty below the configured limit, wind present

08 · Technical stack

Small, reproducible, honest about what's live.

  • TypeScript on Node 22, run with tsx. No build step.
  • HTTP API: POST /run, live streaming over server-sent events, signed sign-off.
  • Front end: vanilla JS, three.js, GSAP, Lenis, served locally. No CDN.
  • Docker: builds and runs from a clean clone with no keys, no network and no GPU.
  • Config, not constants: every threshold in config/skyledger.json, every region in config/regions/.
  • Providers: AI_PROVIDER=nightshift | nvidia | none.
  • Sample mode: cached public data (Carbon Mapper, EPA GHGRP RY2023) and deterministic agents. Identical results every run.
  • Data: Carbon Mapper (non-commercial terms), US EPA GHGRP, SEC EDGAR.

10 · What's unique, and what we measured

Unique

  • Data never leaves its source: Zetaris joins satellite and regulatory data in place, and the same SQL is shown to the analyst.
  • A World Model for corporate ownership: owner strings resolved into parents with provenance, the gap Zetaris's own docs name.
  • Agents that check agents: the Skeptic blocks and sends work back, and the trace proves it.
  • Real data as the interface: every number on the landing page and in the deck comes from the API.

Measured, not claimed

  • Vision: blind Nemotron headings agreed with the wind on 24 of 64 live readings (38%, about 33% by chance) on 114-pixel quicklooks. So vision is advisory: it can add confidence, never downgrade a case alone.
  • Route disclosure: Nightshift 1.2.7 refuses image requests to Nemotron VL. PlumeReader tries Nightshift first, then calls NVIDIA directly, logging each fallback. Reported to Meterless.

11 · Future enhancements

From a basin to a monitoring service.

Coverage

Every US basin and every reporting year; scheduled refresh from Carbon Mapper and EPA; SEC CIK links for parents.

Accuracy

A published labelled test set and accuracy report; vision on larger scenes or as a data-quality check; H-MEM to learn attribution rules from analyst decisions.

Product

Hosted service with logins and audit trails; alerts when a facility's gap persists; export of signed dossiers for disclosure reviews.

Commercial use needs a data agreement with Carbon Mapper, and a legal review of wording that names companies. Both are part of the plan.
