03 The solution

Every plume becomes a traced, ranged, signed-off dossier.

1 · Find

Federated geo-join of plumes to reporting facilities, queried in place through Zetaris.

2 · Attribute

Wind and plume geometry pick the facility at the plume's head. Nemotron reads the image as a check.

3 · Reconcile

kg/h becomes tonnes per year, always a range, inside the reporting window. Owners resolve to parents.

4 · Challenge and sign

A Skeptic rules out five innocent explanations first. Nothing is shareable until an analyst signs.

Live run: Taylor Gas Plant, 1,360 kg/h, seen 10 of 10 times, 18–30× what it reported

Not a linear chain

The Skeptic sends work back: widen the search (GeoLinker), look again (PlumeReader), or stop annualising (Reconciler). Loops are bounded.

Numbers come from code

Distances, unit conversion and ratios come from SQL and code. Models read images and word findings; a guard rejects any number the code didn't compute.

Traceable

Every step goes to a hash-chained JSONL trace that streams live to the console. Editing any line breaks the chain.

05 The Skeptic

Before a word is written, five rival explanations must fall.

R1

Wrong facility

Another site fits the plume head and wind, or the nearest reporter is over 1 km away.

R2

One-off release

Fewer than 2 sightings in the reporting window blocks any yearly total.

R3

Not required to report

No subparts, no CH₄ filed, or no reporter at the plume head.

R4

Uncovered equipment

Only basin-level reports nearby, or no Subpart W coverage.

R5

Bad measurement

No rate, uncertainty above the limit, or no wind.

08 Sponsor technology · Zetaris

Zetaris is the data layer: joined in place, never copied.

  • Three public sources registered as Zetaris Lightning tables: Carbon Mapper sources, GHGRP facilities, GHGRP CH₄ by subpart.
  • One federated geo-join per plume through the REST SQL API: bounding box plus haversine distance, where the data lives.
  • SourceMapper discovers the reachable tables and row counts at the start of every run.
  • Evidence: the exact SQL and a rows hash in every dossier, so an analyst can rerun it.
  • Verified: Zetaris equals the offline cache for 60 / 60 plumes, and the agents' queries appear in Zetaris's own history.
SQL Workspace: a live join across the skyledger tables
History: the geo-joins SkyLedger's agents ran

09 Sponsor technology · Meterless and NVIDIA

Meterless runs the models and the ownership graph. Nemotron reads and writes.

Meterless Nightshift

The primary AI provider, built to Meterless's provider contract (headers, discovery, token handling, Auto Detect). The NVIDIA key lives inside Nightshift, not in our app. Contract checks pass.

Meterless World Model

The facility → owner → parent graph, with provenance on every write, merged spellings and a review queue. Meterless's conformance suite: 8 / 8. Sign-off uses Scout Intent's contract format.

NVIDIA Nemotron

3.5 Lightning via Nightshift writes each dossier under a number guard. 3.5 Super VL reads the plume image blind: 38% agreement with the wind (chance ≈ 33%), so it's advisory. VL fallback to NVIDIA's API is disclosed and logged.

10 Technical stack, and what we measured

Small, reproducible, and honest about what's live.

  • TypeScript on Node 22, run with tsx. No build step, no GPU.

  • Config, not constants: every threshold in config/skyledger.json, regions in config/regions/.
